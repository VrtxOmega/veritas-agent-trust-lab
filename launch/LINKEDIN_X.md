challenge where you judge six plausible agent decisions before seeing what

monitoring.

label collection. You can download your calibration record or explicitly
contribute it publicly on GitHub.

independent audit, or execution authority. I also opened two bounded $750
founding pilots for teams that want one real agent workflow mapped and
attacked.
